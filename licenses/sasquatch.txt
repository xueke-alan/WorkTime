Copyright David W. Skinner (sasquatch@bentonrea.com).
Author page and original text downloads:
https://www.onlinespiele-sammlung.de/sokoban/sokobangames/skinner/
Author distribution terms: "These sets may be freely distributed provided they remain properly credited."
Unchanged source texts s1.txt through s7.txt are preserved as sasquatch-1.txt
through sasquatch-7.txt in assets/data/sources. Original geometry and numbers
are preserved. The generated bank records each source URL and SHA-256.
Sasquatch VII levels 11 and 12 are marked as SokHard derivations in the
original source; neither is included in the selected playable bank.

Historical solution data: Guy Langston / SokoSolve contributors.
https://github.com/guylangston/SokoSolve/blob/4045224e65832e5475d9c1c781251b5c3c4d6ac0/data/Lib/solutions.json
An unchanged copy is preserved as assets/data/sources/sasquatch-solutions.json.
Its repository license is GNU GPL version 2; see licenses/sokosolve.txt.
Imported historical solutions retain their SolutionId and search-node counts.
Solutions without a SolutionId were computed by the WorkTime offline search.
All selected paths are replay-verified and are not claimed to be optimal.
Sokoban source maps remain credited to David W. Skinner, not public domain.
UI, vector artwork and offline search utilities were implemented separately.

The playable bank includes 62 Sasquatch levels and 78 Microban levels, for
140 levels in seven locally assessed groups of 20. The previous 100 playable
levels retain their stable IDs, original maps and solutions; menu numbers
change with the new ordering. The seven labels/ranges reference:
https://aymericdupeloux.wixsite.com/sokoban/post/welcome
This is a classification reference only: no Cosmos levels were redistributed.
