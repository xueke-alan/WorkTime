An unchanged copy of the source text is preserved in assets/data/sources/microban.txt.
The imported data records its SHA-256. The bank selects 78 verified puzzles
from source levels 1–100, preserving original geometry and source numbers.
The original 60 levels retain their IDs, maps, solutions and proven minimum
push counts. Added solutions are replay-verified and are not claimed optimal.
The combined Microban / Sasquatch bank is ordered by local replay metrics in
seven groups of 20. The Cosmos seven-tier labels and ranges are a naming
reference, not an attribution of author ratings to these levels.
Levels with third-party derivation notes at the end of the original collection
are outside this range and are not imported.
This is credited redistribution, not a claim that the levels are public domain.
Game code, UI, solver and translations were implemented separately.
