An unchanged copy of the source text is preserved in assets/data/sources/microban.txt.
The imported data records its SHA-256. The importer selects 60 solvable puzzles
from source levels 1–100, preserving original geometry and source numbers, and
orders them by minimum pushes. Levels with third-party derivation notes at the
end of the original collection are outside this range and are not imported.
This is credited redistribution, not a claim that the levels are public domain.
Game code, UI, solver and translations were implemented separately.
