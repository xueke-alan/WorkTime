Piper browser inference
======================
This application implements Piper ONNX inference with ONNX Runtime Web and
phonemizer.js (eSpeak NG). It does not bundle the Piper Python/C++ engine.

Voice snapshots: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices

UK voice: en_GB-jenny_dioco-medium (single speaker)

and dataset license references. Jenny Dioco's dataset:
https://github.com/dioco-group/jenny-tts-dataset
The repository's top-level MIT metadata does not replace individual voice terms.

ONNX Runtime Web notices are also included in vocabulary.txt.

Model audio is synthesized locally; British/American labels identify the
selected voice and phonemizer settings, not independently certified accuracy.
